## What the model is

Source-available, not open source:

- The source is **publicly readable**, for transparency and for learning from.
- The source is **not freely usable**. Reading it grants no right to ship it, fork it into
  another product, or build on it.

You may read, study, learn from, report bugs against and propose changes to Aestra. You
may not use it in your own software, redistribute it, or build a derivative product from
it, without written permission.
