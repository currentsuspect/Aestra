## Analysis

**Hypothesis was 40-60 Mf/s. Actual: 27.40 Mf/s.** Why the gap?

The 8-tap inner loop is so short that the overhead of the table lookup
(phase index calculation, half-phase check, table pointer fetch) dominates
relative to the compute work. With 64 taps, the table lookup is amortized
over 64 multiply-adds. With 8 taps, the lookup cost is 8x more significant
proportionally.

That said, 3.06x is still a meaningful win. Sinc8 TURBO (27.4 Mf/s) is
now faster than Sinc64 original (1.98 Mf/s) and approaches Cubic territory
(46 Mf/s) while delivering ~100dB SNR vs Cubic's ~80dB.
