## CI Stress Test Results (AVX2, Sinc8 TURBO, ~100dB SNR)

| Clips | Median | P99 | Budget Usage | Status |
|-------|--------|-----|-------------|--------|
| **16** | **0.06ms** | **0.07ms** | **1.2%** | **✅** |
| 32 | 0.14ms | 0.15ms | 2.5% | ✅ |
| 64 | 0.31ms | 0.35ms | 5.7% | ✅ |
| 128 | 0.72ms | 0.74ms | 13.5% | ✅ |

**Budget**: 5.33ms per callback (256 samples @ 48kHz)
**Target**: <70% budget (3.73ms)

## Honest Assessment

- **16 clips uses 1.2% of callback budget** — enormous headroom on CI hardware
- **Breaking point**: ~660 clips (extrapolated from 13.5% at 128)
- **Real-world capacity**: Will be lower than CI numbers due to plugin
  processing, mixing, disk I/O, and UI overhead
- **The claim "pure sinc DAW" is defensible** at 16 clips with Sinc8 TURBO
