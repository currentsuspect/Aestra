## Analysis

**Sinc16Turbo at 29.35 Mf/s — slower than Sinc8Turbo at 43.46 Mf/s.**

This is counterintuitive: 16 taps should amortize the fixed table lookup
overhead better than 8 taps. The explanation is **L1 cache pressure**:

- Sinc8 table: 8KB (128 × 8 × 4 bytes) — fits comfortably in L1 alongside
  sample data access
- Sinc16 table: 16KB (256 × 16 × 4 bytes) — half of a typical 32KB L1,
  competing with sample data for cache lines

The 16KB table + the 16-tap sample access pattern (32 floats = 128 bytes
per interpolation) exceeds the L1 working set, causing more cache misses
per interpolation than the 8-tap case.

**Practical implication**: Sinc16Turbo is still a good middle ground for
quality (~120dB vs Sinc8's ~100dB), but it's not the performance sweet
spot. The sweet spot is Sinc8Turbo — ~100dB SNR at 43 Mf/s, close to
Cubic speed with 20dB more SNR.
