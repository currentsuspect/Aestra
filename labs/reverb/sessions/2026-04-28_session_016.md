### Key Insight

Even with **k=0.00** (no decorrelation), the FDN itself produces negative late
correlation on dense material:
- chord stab: -0.276
- mix bus: -0.558

This means the extreme negative correlation is **partly an FDN characteristic**,
not purely caused by the decorrelation step. The decorrelation amplifies it, but
cannot be the sole fix.

### Why k=0.30 Was Chosen

1. **Significant improvement over k=0.60**: chord stab improves from -0.826 to
   -0.679; mix bus improves from -0.915 to -0.835.

2. **Still provides meaningful width**: Impulse late correlation is 0.820,
   which is still much wider than the pre-Session-011 baseline of 0.934.

3. **Transient material is healthy**: Snare is near zero (-0.019), bright ping
   is moderately wide (+0.553).

4. **Not an overcorrection**: k=0.20-0.25 would further reduce width but with
   diminishing returns on the dense-material problem.

5. **Clean value**: 50% reduction from 0.60 is simple and defensible.
