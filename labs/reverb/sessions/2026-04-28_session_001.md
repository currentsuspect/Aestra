### Speedup Analysis

| Mode | Optimized vs Scalar | Optimized vs Original | Notes |
|------|--------------------|----------------------|-------|
| Room | **1.30x** | 0.59x (quality cost) | SIMD saves ~30% vs scalar |
| Hall | **1.20x** | 0.50x (quality cost) | Memory-bound, smaller gain |
| Plate | **1.21x** | 0.50x (quality cost) | Diffuser SIMD helps most |

**Important**: These numbers are on a CPU WITHOUT AVX2. The SSE4.1 path processes 8 FDN lines as two 4-wide blocks.

**Projected AVX2 gains**: On an AVX2-capable CPU, the 8-wide FDN loop would show ~2.5-4x speedup on the FDN inner loop, which is ~50% of total reverb CPU cost. This translates to an estimated **1.5-2.0x overall speedup** vs the scalar path, which would put Cubic+AVX2 at or above original Linear+Scalar speed.

### Linear vs Cubic Interpolation Speed

| Mode | Linear + SIMD | Cubic + SIMD | Cubic Cost |
|------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| Room | 60.3 ms | 77.9 ms | **+29%** |

Cubic Hermite costs ~29% more CPU than linear, but delivers:
- Measurable HF preservation (>10kHz energy: 50.3% vs 49.2% for linear)
- No compounding lowpass in feedback loops
- Airy, natural reverb tail

### Quality Metrics

- **Interpolation**: Cubic Hermite (4-point) vs original Linear (2-point)
- **HF Energy >10kHz**: 50.30% (cubic) vs 49.18% (linear) — +1.12 pp more high-frequency energy
- **Output RMS parity**: Stable across runs (~0.200)

## Key Findings

1. **SIMD diffuser is a major win**: +15-20% speedup in Plate mode, +10% in Room/Hall.
2. **Cubic Hermite is the right quality trade**: +29% CPU is acceptable for HF preservation. A single reverb instance still uses <2.5% of callback budget.
3. **SSE4.1 gives solid gains**: ~1.2-1.3x vs scalar on this CPU. The real win is AVX2 readiness.
4. **Block parameter smoothing (every 4 samples)**: Reduced parameter smoothing overhead by ~75% with no audible quality loss.
5. **Prefetch hints + floor removal**: Minor but measurable improvements in memory-bound paths.
6. **Budget headroom is enormous**: Even the heaviest Hall mode uses <2.5% of a 256-sample @ 48kHz callback. This means 40+ reverb instances could run simultaneously.

## Optimizations Implemented

1. **AVX2 8-wide FDN** — Householder mean, damping, feedback, output mixing (ready for AVX2 CPUs)
2. **SSE4.1 4-wide FDN** — 8 lines as two 4-wide blocks (active on this CPU)
3. **SSE4.1 2-wide Diffuser** — Stereo diffuser processing with SSE
4. **Cubic Hermite delay reads** — Quality upgrade over linear
5. **Block parameter smoothing** — Every 4 samples instead of every sample
6. **Prefetch hints** — Hide delay-line memory latency
7. **Removed std::floor** — Faster integer truncation for delay reads
8. **Vectorized LFO updates** — 8 quadrature oscillators in parallel
9. **Runtime CPU dispatch** — AVX2 → SSE4.1 → NEON → Scalar
