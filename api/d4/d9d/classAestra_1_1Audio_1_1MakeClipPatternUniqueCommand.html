Detailed Description

Give one Playlist clip its own pattern identity, preserving contents.

Works for MIDI and audio patterns alike: the pattern data is cloned under a new id ("... copy" naming follows PatternManager::clonePattern) and the clip is repointed at the clone. Other clips keep referencing the original.
