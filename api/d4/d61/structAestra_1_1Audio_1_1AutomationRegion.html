Detailed Description

A movable, copyable stretch of a curve: an automation "clip" (V8-A7).

A region is a closed span of beats on one curve, tight to its points: startBeat is its first point, endBeat its last. The points inside the span ARE the region; nothing else is stored, so the engine still evaluates the curve exactly as before and a curve without regions plays and saves as it always has. Points outside every span are loose, as drawn by hand. Regions of one curve never overlap.
