4// The one layout every Settings page uses (V82-P12, canvas-2 board 2): small-caps section captions, then rows of
5// label | control | hint, a hairline between rows, and the controls lined up in one column. Pages describe their
6// rows in order; the form places the controls, paints the captions, labels and lines, and scrolls when a page is
7// taller than the dialog.
