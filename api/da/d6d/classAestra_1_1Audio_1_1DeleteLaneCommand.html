Detailed Description

Delete a lane and its clips (FD-14 phase-5 lane management).

Owned take lanes are detached from their Track before removal; unowned lanes are removed outright. Undo restores the lane with its ORIGINAL id, position, and ownership (createLaneWithId + moveLaneToIndex + moveLaneWithinTrack), so an undone deletion is identity-stable AND stays in its playlist/track order. Deleting a lane does not delete its patterns — same policy as Delete Clip.
