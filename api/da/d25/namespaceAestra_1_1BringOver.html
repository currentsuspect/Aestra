Detailed Description

Bring over (SPEC-017, V81-V1): take chosen tracks and patterns from another version into the open one.

Nothing merges by itself. The plan lists what the other version has and how it compares by name; the command adds exactly what was chosen and goes back out in one undo step.

Audio and pattern clips travel with the audio they play. A track whose patterns play instruments the open version does not have is listed as blocked rather than imported half-working. Imported tracks arrive routed to Master: mixer channels are not part of what is brought over.
