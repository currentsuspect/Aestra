Detailed Description

The slider of the sharp-cornered Aestra look: a flat track, a flat fill and a square thumb, no glow.

Mechanics are NUISlider's (drag, Shift for fine, double-click back to the default). A bipolar slider fills from the zero point, so a pan or a pitch reads as a distance from centre. Used by the floating editor panels.
