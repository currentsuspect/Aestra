Detailed Description

The sound strip at the top of an open sampler row (V81-AR1, FD-27 point 5): step to the previous or next sound in the same folder and hear it in the pattern at once.

Swapping is gap-free (V81-E3), so notes already sounding finish on the old sound and the next hit is the new one.

The neighbour rule is pure (siblings, stepped): the audio files beside the current one, sorted by name, and a wrap-around step through them.
