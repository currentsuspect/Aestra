Detailed Description

The mixer's navigator for sessions that overflow the panel (V81-M1, FD-28 point 7).

One tick per channel, in the strips' order and proportion: its colour on top and a live level inside. A bracket marks what is on screen; drag it, or click a tick to centre that channel. Group labels above the ticks jump to a group. A red flag means clipped since reset, a green one soloed, and both stay visible while the channel is off-screen. The panel shows it only when channels overflow.

Positions are in strip-row coordinates (0 .. contentWidth), so the navigator maps them linearly.
