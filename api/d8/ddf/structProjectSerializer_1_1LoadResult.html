size_t ProjectSerializer::LoadResult::legacyAudioPatternsSplit {0}

Pre-v3 audio patterns the loader had to split into one pattern per destination channel.

Non-zero means patterns exist in memory that are not in the file, which is why such a load reports Transformed.
