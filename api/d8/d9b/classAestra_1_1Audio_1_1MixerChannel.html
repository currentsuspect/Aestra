Detailed Description

Mixer Channel - Dedicated routing and DSP entity (v3.0)

Refactored from legacy Track.h. Stripped of timeline/clip storage. Focuses on:

  • Routing (MixerBus)
  • Volume, Pan, Mute, Solo
  • DSP Effects & Quality Settings
