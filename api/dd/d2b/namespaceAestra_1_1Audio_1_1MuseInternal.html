Detailed Description

The seam the families and the dispatcher share.

Only the NEW surface lives in here. The helpers further down stay directly in Aestra::Audio, unqualified, because every moved verb body calls them by their bare names — makeError(id, ...), isQueryVerb(verb) — and a lookup that has to walk out of MuseInternal to find them still works, whereas renaming the calls to match a tidier namespace would have meant editing all of them.
