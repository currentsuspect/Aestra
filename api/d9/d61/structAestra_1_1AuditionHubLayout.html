Detailed Description

Where everything in the Audition hub sits (FD-26, V81-A): the navigation at the left, the player bar along the bottom, and the page between them with its shelves of tiles.

Pure geometry, so the hit-testing is tested without a window.

The page of one mix or reference: a hero with its cover and the compare control, the waveform of both sides, the numbers beside it, and (with a reference chosen) a rail at the right for the level match.

The item page's tabs: Overview is the waveform and the numbers; the others look at one side of the comparison.
