Detailed Description

Live momentary (400 ms) and short-term (3 s) loudness (EBU R128 / ITU-R BS.1770, V81-M2).

Fed the K-weighted energy of each sample (left squared plus right squared) by the audio thread, which is the only writer. Energy is averaged in 100 ms sub-blocks; momentary is the mean of the last four, and short-term the mean of the last thirty. Both read as silence until a full momentary window exists, and the short-term value ramps in over the first three seconds (it averages what it has), so a meter is never dark for three seconds after play.

Any thread may read the two getters. A reset is requested from any thread and applied by the writer at its next sub-block boundary, so the window never races the audio thread.
