◆ toWire()

std::string Aestra::Audio::HostTransport::toWire ( ) const
inline

The snapshot as trailing tokens on a sandbox PROCESS line: " T <flags> <num> <den> <sample> <tempo> <rate> <ppq> <bar ppq> <loop start> <loop end>".

Flags: 1 valid, 2 playing, 4 looping. Doubles go as the hex of their bits, so no locale (a plugin may call setlocale) can turn a decimal point into a comma between the app and the helper. Empty when the snapshot is not valid.
