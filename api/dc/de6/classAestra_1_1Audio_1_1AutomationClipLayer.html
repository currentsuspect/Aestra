Detailed Description

The automation clips of one lane: drawn as headed bands, dragged by their grip to move, Ctrl-dragged to copy, right-clicked on the header for Duplicate / Split Here / Delete (V8-A7).

Only the grip (a small tab at the clip's start) and a right-click on the header are the layer's. Every other press, including one on the rest of the header, is point editing's, and a point under the pointer always wins, so adding or grabbing points near the top of the lane works as before.

The operations are AutomationRegions.h; this is only the gesture on top of them. Every edit is one undo step: the lane's curves are snapshotted at the press, edited live while dragging, and pushed as an EditAutomationCurvesCommand on release. A curve that has points but no stored clips shows one implicit clip over all its points.
