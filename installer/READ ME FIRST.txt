AESTRA v0.8.2 preview (early access)
====================================

Thank you for trying Aestra this early. This is a preview of the next version: newer
than the one shown in the videos, with VST3 and CLAP plugin support built in.
It is an alpha. The main parts work, some parts are rough, and some features are
still being built.

STARTING IT
-----------
Windows: unzip the folder anywhere, then open Aestra.exe.
         Windows may warn about an unknown publisher ("Windows protected your PC").
         Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The app is not code-signed yet.
Linux:   extract the folder, then run ./Aestra from inside it.
         It needs SDL2, FreeType, ALSA and libsecret, which most desktops already have.

Keep everything in the folder together: AestraPluginHost(.exe), AestraUpdater(.exe)
and the AestraAssets folder must stay next to the app.

UPDATES
-------
You should only need to download Aestra once. Open Settings > Updates, choose the
Early channel, and paste the founder key from your email. Aestra then checks for new
builds by itself and offers "Download and install" when one is ready. Your projects
are not touched.

PLUGINS
-------
Aestra runs every plugin inside AestraPluginHost, so a plugin that crashes cannot take
Aestra down. It looks in the usual folders
(Windows: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and \CLAP).
Plugin support is new. Tested so far: Surge XT, Vital, Dexed, TAL-NoiseMaker,
Airwindows and LSP. Others may load fine or may not; that is exactly what we want
to hear about. Plugin windows have not yet been seen on a real Windows desktop by
anyone outside the studio, so you may be the first.

WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
- Windows has had less testing than Linux, so you are more likely to hit problems there.
- Save often. Projects made with this alpha may not open in later versions.
- Crashes can happen. If Aestra crashes, the Versions panel offers a recovery card
  the next time you open the project.

TELLING US WHAT HAPPENED
------------------------
Reply to the email that brought you this download, or write to hello@aestra.studio.
The most useful report says what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and
includes a screenshot if you can. For a plugin: its name, VST3 or CLAP, and whether
it loaded, made sound, opened its window, and came back after save and reopen.
Short notes are fine.
